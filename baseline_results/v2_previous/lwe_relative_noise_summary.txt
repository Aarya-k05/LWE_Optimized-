Note: absolute noise comes from integer noise injected every step (F.e / 2^24 for
control, e / 2^16 per state entry), so it does not decay while the
signal does. Per-step relative error therefore rises as the regulator
converges; peak-relative and horizon-relative are the stable figures.
Same plaintext baseline is assumed for CKKS (verified upstream by the
comparison script). No security claim: custom scheme, not standard HE.
